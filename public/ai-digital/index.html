The practice

Start with the work people do.

Digital change involves workflows, skills and decisions. We examine these together, helping teams identify where technology can help and what they need to adopt it responsibly.

Our work connects institutional roadmaps, AI education and service design, with attention to access, local capacity and long-term ownership.

Who we work with

  • Public institutions
  • Regional bodies
  • Development agencies
  • Universities and learning institutions
  • Service providers
  • Civil society organisations

What we do

Four ways
we can help.

Start with a defined question or bring these capabilities together around a wider institutional challenge.

01 / Area of work

Digital transformation roadmaps

Map processes, identify priorities and sequence change around institutional needs and capacity.

02 / Area of work

Digital readiness and skills

Assess readiness and design learning programmes that help teams use technology with confidence.

03 / Area of work

AI capability and education

Develop curricula and learning pathways grounded in practical applications and considered judgement.

04 / Area of work

Digital product and service design

Co-design services for the people using them, including settings with limited connectivity or digital access.

How we work

Context first.
Practical next steps.

An approach shaped around the institution, its people and the decision at hand.

  1. 01

    Understand the work

    Listen to staff and users. Map processes, constraints and the decisions that matter.

  2. 02

    Assess readiness

    Examine skills, systems and institutional capacity before agreeing priorities.

  3. 03

    Design together

    Develop options with the people who will use and maintain them.

  4. 04

    Build capability

    Connect implementation with learning, knowledge transfer and clear ownership.
