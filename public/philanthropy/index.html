The practice

Connect funding choices with how institutions work.

A funding strategy takes shape through budgets, governance, relationships and everyday decisions. We study how these processes work and where they support or constrain an organisation's aims.

Our advisory work brings together institutional research, evidence review and strategic prioritisation to help funders examine their choices and the assumptions behind them.

Who we work with

  • Foundations
  • Intermediary funds
  • Donor groups
  • Corporate giving programmes
  • Product-development partnerships

What we do

Four ways

Start with a defined question or bring these capabilities together around a wider institutional challenge.

01 / Area of work

Institutional power mapping

02 / Area of work

Evidence for giving strategies

03 / Area of work

Market and demand research

Investigate needs, demand and adoption considerations that inform programme and product investment.

04 / Area of work

Strategic prioritisation

Compare opportunities, resource requirements and delivery considerations when shaping a funding portfolio.

How we work

Context first.
Practical next steps.

An approach shaped around the institution, its people and the decision at hand.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Clarify the purpose, institutional context and choices the work needs to inform.

  2. 02

    Gather evidence

    Combine research and stakeholder perspectives to examine needs and organisational practice.

  3. 03

    Test assumptions

    Explore theories of change, trade-offs and the practical implications of different options.

  4. 04

    Shape next steps

    Translate findings into priorities and changes that the organisation can take forward.
