How we approach a challenge

From understanding
to action.

01 / Understand

Read the context.

Listen to the people doing the work. Examine evidence, relationships, constraints and the decisions that matter.

02 / Design

Make options tangible.

Shape practical responses with the people who will use them. Test assumptions before making commitments.

03 / Build capability

Leave people able to act.

Connect the work with skills, ownership and learning, so the next step belongs to the institution and its people.
