The studio

Evidence-led.

Present Lab brings research, design and institutional capability together to work on challenges that cross disciplines and sectors.

Our perspective

The context
is part of the work.

We start with how people and institutions actually work: what they know, what they need, what they can change and what gets in the way.

Across our four practices, we connect technical expertise with questions of agency, ownership and implementation. The work may begin with a research question, a policy, a service or a system. It moves forward through evidence, collaboration and practical choices.
