The practice

Make room for people to shape decisions.

Lasting change depends on whose knowledge counts, who sets priorities and who can act. We help organisations examine these questions in their strategy and everyday practice.

Our work combines systems analysis, co-design, facilitation and learning. Methods adapt to the context, capacities and priorities of the people involved.

Who we work with

  • Community organisations
  • Civil society organisations
  • Social movements
  • Intermediaries and ecosystem actors
  • Public institutions
  • Researchers and journalists

How we work

Context first.
Practical next steps.

An approach shaped around the institution, its people and the decision at hand.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Listen to lived experience and explore the issues beneath the immediate problem.

  2. 02

    Define

    Agree whose needs to address and which part of the system to work on.

  3. 03

    Co-design

    Develop practical options and make assumptions visible through early prototypes.

  4. 04

    Test and learn

    Gather feedback, examine what happened and decide what to adapt, develop or stop.
